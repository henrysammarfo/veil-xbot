## Rules (from X open algorithm — avoid negative signals)

- **Do:** video, reply-worthy hooks, quote-tweets, 15 replies in 30 min
- **Don't:** spam links in community main post, hashtag soup, tag 10 accounts
- **First week:** 80% replies in communities, 20% own posts
- **Week 2+:** own post + cross-post same video to 2 communities (different caption)

## Post order for brand-new channel

1. **Day 0:** Profile pic + bio + pin nothing yet
2. **Day 1:** FIRST VIDEO POST on main feed (see `npm run launch veil`)
3. **Day 1 +5 min:** Reply on your post with link
4. **Day 1 +30 min:** Post same hook as **text reply** in Build in Public community (no link — link in follow-up)
5. **Day 2–7:** `engage-batch` daily + 1 community comment/day
